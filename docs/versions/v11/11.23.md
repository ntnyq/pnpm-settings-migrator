# pnpm 11.23

Compared with pnpm 11.22.x. Patch changes within 11.23.x are listed at their exact release boundaries.

## Added and changed fields

- `registries` gains URL-keyed declarations with `serverType`, `scopes`,
  `prefix`, and `supportsTimeField`. Legacy scope-to-URL maps still work.
- `virtualStoreType: global | project` is the canonical replacement for
  `enableGlobalVirtualStore`.
- `audit` and `update` become the documented names; `auditConfig`, `auditLevel`,
  and `updateConfig` remain readable legacy forms.
- `namedRegistries` is deprecated in favor of URL-keyed `registries`.

The migrator normalizes deprecated forms when `--replace-deprecated` is enabled,
preserving canonical values and keeping credentials in their original location.
Unknown or non-camelCase workspace settings now produce upstream warnings.
